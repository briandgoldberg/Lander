Privacy

Lander is a small game: you write a flight path in JavaScript and we fly it between planets. This page says what we keep and why.

What we store

  • Your Google account email and name, received when you sign in with Google. We use them to identify your account, show your name on the leaderboard, and contact a winner. We never see your Google password.
  • Your flight paths, the code you submit for a launch, and the results of each launch. The winning flight path of a game is published on the site when the game ends, as the rules say.
  • Launch credits and referrals: how many launches you have, your referral code, and which account referred you.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your email with anyone.
  • We don't run ads or third-party trackers.
  • We don't ask for any Google data beyond your basic profile.

Where it lives

Accounts and launches are stored with Supabase, and the site runs on Vercel. Your browser keeps a copy of your draft flight path and a referral code in local storage so they survive a reload.

Deleting your account

Email briandgoldberg@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we'll delete your account and its launches. A published winning flight path stays published.
