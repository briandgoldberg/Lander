Privacy

Lander is a small game: you plan a flight with a few dials and we fly it between planets. This page says what we keep and why.

What we store

  • Your Google account email and name, received when you sign in with Google. We use them to identify your account, show your name on the leaderboard, and contact a winner. We never see your Google password.
  • Your flights: the dial settings you launch with and the result of each launch. Flights are public: anyone can watch any replay and see its settings.
  • Shots and referrals: how many shots you have used, your referral code, and which account referred you.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your email with anyone.
  • We don't run ads or third-party trackers.
  • We don't ask for any Google data beyond your basic profile.

Where it lives

Accounts and launches are stored with Supabase, and the site runs on Vercel. Your browser keeps your current dial settings and a referral code in local storage so they survive a reload.

Deleting your account

Email briandgoldberg@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we'll delete your account and its launches.
