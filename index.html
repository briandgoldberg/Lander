        API reference
        
    
          
  • world.sun {mass, radius} · world.bodies[k] {name, mass, radius, orbitR, omega, angle0, period, hill} · world.start, world.target are indices into bodies · world.rocket {maxThrust, fuel, padAngle} · world.deadline · world.safeLandingSpeed · world.hints {transferTime, synodic, hohmannDv, escapeStart, escapeTarget}
    • 
          
  • Units: star mass 1, G 1, the innermost orbit is about 1. Planets move counterclockwise on circular orbits around a fixed star. Fuel is a delta-v budget: throttle 1 burns maxThrust per time unit. Steps are adaptive, finer near planets and during burns.
    • 
          
  • controller(state) is called every step. state = {t, x, y, vx, vy, fuel, onPad, bodies:[{x,y,vx,vy}]}. Return {throttle, dx, dy}: throttle 0 to 1, (dx, dy) the thrust direction in world coordinates, any length.
    • 
          
  • sim.fly(controller) flies a test flight and returns {outcome, body, fuelUsed, time, closestApproach, landingSpeed}. Outcomes: landed, crashed (body says into what), burned (the star), lost, timeout. sim.timeLeft() is your remaining budget in ms. sim.flights counts test flights.
    • 
          
  • Landed means touching the target planet at a speed relative to it of at most safeLandingSpeed. Most fuel left among safe landings wins.
