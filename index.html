    Sandbox entry

    JavaScript. Define bot(world, sim) and return a controller. The sandbox runs your code in a sealed worker with no network and a 10 second clock, then flies the controller you returned through its own physics. That flight is your entry. The starter below finds the launch window and reaches the target planet. It does not survive the arrival. Landing is your job.

    
    
    
      API reference
      
      
        
    • world.sun {mass, radius} · world.bodies[k] {name, mass, radius, orbitR, omega, angle0, period, hill} · world.start, world.target are indices into bodies · world.rocket {maxThrust, fuel, padAngle} · world.deadline · world.safeLandingSpeed · world.hints {transferTime, synodic, hohmannDv, escapeStart, escapeTarget}
      • 
        
    • Units: sun mass 1, G 1, the innermost orbit is about 1. Planets move counterclockwise on circular orbits around a fixed sun. Fuel is a delta-v budget: throttle 1 burns maxThrust per time unit. Steps are adaptive, finer near planets.
      • 
        
    • controller(state) is called every step. state = {t, x, y, vx, vy, fuel, onPad, bodies:[{x,y,vx,vy}]}. Return {throttle, dx, dy}: throttle 0 to 1, (dx, dy) the thrust direction in world coordinates, any length.
      • 
        
    • sim.fly(controller) flies a test flight and returns {outcome, body, fuelUsed, time, closestApproach, landingSpeed}. Outcomes: landed, crashed (body says into what), burned (the sun), lost, timeout. sim.timeLeft() is your remaining budget in ms. sim.flights counts test flights.
      • 
        
    • Landed means touching the target planet at a speed relative to it of at most safeLandingSpeed. Lowest fuel used among landings wins.
      •
